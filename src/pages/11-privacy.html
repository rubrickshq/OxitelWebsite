Privacy policy

Last updated 7 September 2026.

Who we are

Oxitel ("we", "us") operates the website oxitel.net and provides wholesale voice services to businesses. This policy explains what personal data we collect through the website, why, and what rights you have.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, company, work email, country, area of interest and the message you send us.
  • Email: anything you send to our sales or NOC addresses.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and performance.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to provide rates and set up services you ask for, and to keep the website secure. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and, if you become a customer, for the duration of our relationship plus the period required by law. Server logs are retained for a short period by our hosting provider.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the website and email (hosting and email delivery), under contracts that restrict how they use the data. We may disclose data where required by law.

Cookies

The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and fonts are served from our own domain — nothing is loaded from third-party servers when you visit.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how we use it. Email sales@oxitel.net and we will respond within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sales@oxitel.net.
