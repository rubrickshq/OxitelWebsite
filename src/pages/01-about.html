About Oxitel

A new carrier with an experienced team

We started Oxitel to do one thing well: move voice traffic to the destinations our customers care about, at a price that makes sense, on routes we actually control.

Our story

Built around the opposite of what frustrated us

Oxitel was founded in 2026 by a team that has spent years on both sides of the wholesale voice business — buying routes, selling routes, and sitting in the NOC at 3 a.m. when a destination goes down. We saw the same problems repeat: rates padded by too many middlemen, routes that test well and degrade a week later, and support tickets that disappear into a queue.

So we built a carrier around the opposite. Direct terminations in the African markets we know best. Routes tested continuously, not once. A NOC that answers around the clock.

We are a young company, and we are honest about that. What we bring is an experienced team, routes we control, and the discipline to run them properly.

What we believe

Three things we don't compromise on

Direct terminations

Our own routes into the markets we specialise in, not just resold capacity. That is where the pricing and the quality come from.

Continuously tested routes

We test suppliers and destinations on an ongoing basis. If a route slips, we reroute before your customers notice.

24/7 NOC

Engineers available around the clock for interconnect issues, route changes and escalations. Real people, not an auto-reply.

Where we operate

Direct in Africa. Connected worldwide.

Direct termination focus: Nigeria, Zambia, Zimbabwe, South Africa and Tanzania. Interconnects across the Middle East, South East Asia, Australia, Latin America, the CIS region and Europe.
