Why CLI matters

No caller ID, no answer, no callback

When caller ID is missing or wrong, people don't pick up, and they can't call back. For call centres, that is lost conversions. For businesses, it is lost trust. Premium CLI routes solve this by sending traffic only over direct terminations that preserve the caller ID end to end.
