Our story

Oxitel was founded in {{ site.founded }} by a team that has spent years on both sides of the wholesale voice business — buying routes, selling routes, and sitting in the NOC at 3 a.m. when a destination goes down. So we built a carrier around the opposite. Direct terminations in the African markets we know best. Routes tested continuously, not once. A NOC that answers around the clock.
